Instructions

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B on Chrome/Firefox)
  2. Drag the "Add to Podcast" button above to your bookmarks bar
  3. Navigate to any YouTube video
  4. Click the bookmarklet to open the submission form with the URL pre-filled

How It Works

  • The bookmarklet detects if you're on a YouTube video page
  • It opens this site with the video URL pre-filled
  • You can optionally edit the metadata before submitting
  • Works with youtube.com/watch and youtu.be URLs

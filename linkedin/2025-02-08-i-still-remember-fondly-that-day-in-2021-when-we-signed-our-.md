I still remember fondly that day in 2021 when we signed our partner agreement with a relatively unknown company, Fishtown Analytics, the makers of dbt.

But as they grew and rebranded into dbt Labs, we grew with them, and we built a fantastic team around their offering.

So, it's with a mix of joy and pride that we received the Service Partner of the Year Award yesterday in Vegas!

Thank you!

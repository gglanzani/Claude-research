Data Engineer

Amsterdam, Noord-Holland, Netherlands Full-time Hybrid

About this role

Does This Sound Like You?

You are an engineer with a pragmatic attitude. You are comfortable with the responsibility that comes with putting systems into production. You switch easily between scripting and structured programming in typed programming languages. You understand software engineering, cloud, provisioning, streaming and automation. You know how to build robust systems.

The Xebia Data Division

Xebia Data is one of the divisions of Xebia Netherlands, which is renowned for its expertise in building strong Data and AI practices to deliver value to our clients and their customers. In the past, our team has predicted the daily demand for millions of loaves of bread, built advanced energy-saving algorithms on a national scale, and established data innovation labs at large multinational companies!

You’ll work with the sharpest data engineers in Europe, people who are not only technically brilliant but also generous with their knowledge. We enjoy the benefits of being part of a large organization while maintaining the energetic atmosphere of a scale-up. Our commitment to our community is strong; we are deeply rooted in the local landscape.

What You’ll Be Doing as a Data Engineer Consultant

You will be responsible for implementing the fundamentals of data systems and developing the infrastructure to store, transform, and process data at scale and in the cloud. You’ll ensure secure data storage using the appropriate technologies. You will be surrounded by experts from different fields and learn from different environments and industries. It’s not always easy, but it is always rewarding.

You will work on projects of varying lengths. Some are three weeks, some are one year, most are in between. Every project is different, as is every client. You'll work with the customer in teams mixed by Xebians and the client, and pick up work related to the backbone the team is building on. 


When on-site with clients, you may encounter something that is very difficult to solve. At Xebia we work as a team and we've got your back. Everyone is just a message away and ready to help! We say People First, and we mean it.

Requirements

What are we looking for

The Data Engineer role is a senior position that is integral to our teams, so we ask for at least 3 years of relevant work experience. That said, we’re always open to talent that stands out! If you have unique achievements, like contributing to open-source projects or founding your own startup, we encourage you to apply, no matter your experience level.

This is your dream job if….


✅ If you enjoy working with modern new data (infrastructure) tools such as Airflow, Dagster, Spark, Flink, Iceberg, Docker, Terraform, and more!

✅If you understand the importance of cloud (any of AWS, Azure, or GCP) and know your way around, or want to learn.
✅If you like working for different clients and enjoy learning new environments and meeting new people on a regular basis.
✅ You live in the Netherlands (we are not relocating at the moment)

This is not your dream job if….

❌ If you don't like working under pressure to get things done in the best way possible.

❌ If you do not like working with open-source technology.

❌ If you do not like coding, scripting, or templating.

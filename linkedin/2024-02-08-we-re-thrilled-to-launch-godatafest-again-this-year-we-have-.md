We're thrilled to launch GoDataFest again this year.

We have so many speakers and incredible workshops, but let's see some highlights

- We have Royal FloraHolland 🌼 sharing how they built a simulator that mimics their workforce, buildings, and IT systems. It's better than digital twins, thanks to Dirk Guijt and Daniël Tom
- We have Transactie Monitoring Nederland (TMNL) 💸 sharing how MLOps accelerates their teams, thanks to Axel Goblet and Joost Bosman
- Dexter Energy ☀️ will share their challenges in renewable energy forecasting at scale with their CTO Mehmet Sencer KARADAYI
- Mollie, one of our favorite 🦄, will share their journey from MLOps to LLMOps, with Wessel Huising on stage!
- Rabobank will details how they leverage Databricks. Courtesy of the Stef D. and Alexander Bij

You'll notice that we are asking for a small entrance fee this year.

We don't do it for the money—but to avoid wasting food, which is always painful at this time when many around us can't make ends meet.

If, however, the few euros we're asking are too much for you—just drop me a message, and I'll give you a code to get in free of charge!

Spread the word and don't forget to hit the like button!
